**The idea in one line:** the book, and then the weather it was about (a sky drawn in 1803 by Dalton's friend Luke
Howard), and then the place, Kendal. Each beat of the line gets its own subject, and each world is a period picture.
Film did not exist in 1800, so there is no "clip from 1800". The honest 1800 sky is a print of the time.

### Builder notes (what LayerStage does not do yet)
1. **Timed text on the world.** LayerStage has only a static `caption`. The two diary dates need timed labels: `atSec`,
   `untilSec`, `x`, `y`, `align`, entering by wipe-on (0.6 s) and leaving by MOT-013 un-wipe. Add a small `labels` prop, or
   overlay MOT-013 Labels in the act. Do not attach the dates to the page as carry states: under the page they would
   read as the page's own caption, which means the book standing in for the diary.
2. **Carry tag exit.** A carry state that clears its label currently drops it on one frame. It needs the MOT-013 un-wipe
   (0.4-0.6 s), and the tag must start leaving at least 0.4 s before the page moves. Here the tag starts at 3.20, is fully
   gone at 3.60, and the page starts to sink at 3.60.
3. **Props sketch** (values, not code):
   - `worlds[0]`: `{ atSec: 0, srcId: <new SRC>, kind: "image", size: <scan crop px>, focus: <so the shower sits at [1340, 420]>, light: "day", label: "A DISTANT SHOWER · LUKE HOWARD · 1803", why: … }`

### Rejected options
- **Modern Mixkit sky SRC-040, even tagged TODAY:** Sam rejected it. A sky from today behind a 1793 page has no relation the viewer can see.
- **"A clip from 1800":** film did not exist before the late 1880s, so any such clip would be fake period footage.
- **The page stays and lands beside Kendal on the map:** it would be on screen under "a diary of the weather" and read as the diary (SRC-035 limit). It would also be cut away about 0.5 s after it landed.
- **The map as the first world, behind the book:** it shows Kendal 10 s before it is named. It would also tie the 57-year diary to Kendal, but Dalton kept most of it in Manchester: the title page already says "New College, Manchester", 1793.
- **Whip-pan from sky to map (as in the rejected test):** the move from sky to ground is vertical, not sideways. A dissolve says "the light changes" and is calmer.
- **An inside page of the 1793 book (rain at Kendal and Keswick) as the world:** paper on paper in the same tone, which competes with the subject. It is still the book, not the diary.
- **A page of Dalton's own weather journal:** the best possible relation, but no openly licensed scan was found. Holdings are at the John Rylands Library and the Science Museum; many Dalton manuscripts burned in 1940.
- **Howard's watercolour cloud studies at the Science Museum:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which is non-commercial and so not for a monetised channel.
- **Candle clip SRC-011 ("he writes by candle"):** the register keeps it for the candlelit Kendal evening only, and it is modern.
- **Quaker advice page SRC-034 behind the book ("lives that way"):** a page behind a page has low contrast, and its beats are used by 06+07.
