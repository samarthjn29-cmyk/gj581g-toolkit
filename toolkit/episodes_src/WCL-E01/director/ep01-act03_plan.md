## Changes from the on-screen fact-check (23 Nov 2026, gate1_act3_onscreen_factcheck.md)
- Shot A: the pin stays general on "Manchester". In 1794 the Society met at Cross Street Chapel and moved to George Street only in Dec 1799, so the pin never marks the New College or George Street as where the talk was given. The label NEW COLLEGE · TUTOR · AGE 28 sits on the building marked "Academy", Dawson Street.
- p.28: public/assets/sources/dalton1798_p28.jpg, the SHI copy (Roy G. Neville library); the shelf label and stamp are cropped off.
- Laurel quote: "The face of a laurel-leaf (*Prunus Lauro-cerasus*) is a good match to a stick of red sealing-wax…". The picture is a pressed seal, so no caption calls the picture a stick.
- Prism quote: credited to DALTON, 1798, p.31 (typed; p.31 not shown). If his words are typed, they are "I see only two or at most three distinctions".
- Olive in shot B: taken from a Machado deuteranope simulation of the cut-outs themselves (leaf #4F492A, wax #5F5405 → #574F18).
- Eye: tag "DALTON'S GUESS" while the jelly is blue; red and green both thin out; the lens is drawn as a separate part.
- Shot C (changed): inside the blue-jelly eye the two cut-outs go dull and darker under the blue (his own blue-glass idea), under the DALTON'S GUESS tag. The olive (the modern model) never appears inside his guess.
- Henry p.25: the "pale yellow" vitreous line two lines below is dimmed (it contradicts Ransome p.202 and act 6).
- Spectrum bottom strip: red end dim, not black; one yellow fading from strong to pale under orange, yellow and green; blue under blue, darker blue under purple.
