## Structure moves (for the scripts; no register row)
1. **Contradiction cold open:** 30 s of real clips that disagree ("democracy means voting"; "democracy is more than voting"; "the US isn't a democracy"). Then the host admits: "I know what democracy is, but I don't really know."
   *Ours:* 3-4 sourced lines from real documents or people, set as quote cards, each one contradicting the last. Then Sam's own confusion line.
2. **The definition montage:** about 14 one-line definitions at about 2.4 s each, ending on "What is democracy?" and a title card ("WTF IS DEMOCRACY?", white on black, a red underline).
   *Ours:* the same rhythm with sourced quotes and no invented ones.
3. **The numbered-box device:** a box of numbered black drawers arrives, and the producer "can't open it" until each chapter. Each drawer holds one coloured foam piece, one principle per chapter. At the end the pieces fit together into a cube (The Cube). This open loop runs the whole video.
   *Ours:* BuildUpMap (VIS-048) plus ChapterColourKey (LOK-026). Pieces are collected act by act and fit together in the last act.
4. **Unreadable chart, rebuilt:** "this graphic is extremely hard to read". The real paper's Figure 3 is shown printed, then flipped into a plain bar chart, sorted, on paper texture. One row then gets a yellow marker bar and its bar turns green.
   *Ours:* ChartRebuild (VIS-046).
5. **Misquote check:** a common YouTube comment quoting "Benjamin Franklin" is shown as a comment card over a faded engraving: "Did he actually say that?"
   *Ours:* the misconception beat. The decked-out rule "one misconception said out loud and corrected" gets a picture.
6. **Illustrated metaphor:** "four wolves and a lamb voting" drawn as black silhouettes on white with tiny VOTE signs.
   *Ours:* our own silhouettes; never a real person drawn.
7. **The trade-off turn near the end** (about 89%, 44:47): like sliders on a game character, maxing one principle cuts another ("who has time to go to town meetings? old people, rich people").
   *Ours:* TradeoffSliders (VIS-047). It matches our fairness turn at 75-90%.
8. **Ending:** a plain values statement, then "it admits we won't necessarily get it right, but if we keep trying ... we can." A keeper line, as in our rule.

## Look moves
- **Footage in a card on paper:** camera or video-call footage in a white rounded card with a soft shadow on a paper texture. Two-up calls sit side by side. **Each chapter's frames take that chapter's colour** (blue, red, green, orange).
  *Ours:* the frame colour is LOK-026.
- **A web page as evidence:** a full YouTube or browser page shown in a browser frame, never cropped to the video alone, so the source is visible.
- **Clip subtitles:** yellow serif subtitles on news clips, with the key word in a second colour. A grey source line sits under each clip.
- **The set:** a warm practical-lit studio (lamps, globe, maps, radio), with symmetrical wide shots for the ending.
  *Ours:* the stills format has no host footage. This is noted only.
