# Johnny Harris: how things come on and how they leave (study, 23 Nov 2026)

Sam, 23 Nov 2026: "Can you watch 5 videos on Johnny harris channel for their cinematics to learn and apply those to our channel? Pay attention to how new objects are brought in on to the screen and how they leave the screen."

The videos were watched muted in the built-in browser and are for analysis only: no transcripts or frames are kept. The method is the one used for the Vox study:
1. A frame sheet across each video.
2. A change scan of the graphics passages.
3. Frame bursts 0.08-0.5 s apart around each event.

## How things come on
1. **The thing arrives by its own motion.**
   - A bus drives in from the right edge across the US map (about 1.3 s, slowing to a stop).
   - A hand slides in from the left holding a pouch (about 0.4 s), and only once it has landed does the pouch open to show the coins.
   - Objects do what they would do in life, then do their one action.
2. **Converge, then name.** Two objects in circles drift in from the frame edges towards the centre. Then the word BELIEF types on above them, and the gold bar glints after it lands.
3. **The camera brings them in.**
   - A long painted panorama sits on a paper strip, and the camera trucks along it, so new figures enter from the edge.
   - On every map the camera never stops: a slow push the whole time.
4. **Light travels.** On the 3D relief map, the Nile lights up along its course, upstream, as the camera follows (about 4 s). The line is a glow with a bright core, not a drawn stroke.
5. **Arrive in accent, then settle.** The place name "Khartoum" lands on the drone footage in red and cools to white about 0.6-1.2 s later. The eye goes to it, then it sits quietly in the picture.
6. **Ink spreads.** On the Iran map, each power's zone spreads over the country like ink bleeding from several points, blotchy, in about 1.3 s. Lines to small icons are dashed.
7. **Drill down.** A crowd of a few hundred figures:
   - The camera pushes in three times (about 0.7 s each).
   - At each step the group at the centre recolours (tan, then 7 green, then 1 blue) and the rest go pale.
8. **Macro to whole.** The camera starts close on a chart's title and one part of its line, then pulls back to the full chart while the line keeps drawing.
9. **The archive gate.**
   - Archive pictures are shown in a rounded "projector gate" on black, each fitted to its own shape, about 1-2 s each.
   - Between slides there are one or two black frames, like a slide projector changing.
   - When the story steps into the archive, the gate grows to full frame.

## How things leave
1. **Old marks give way to the new.** On the Iran map, the small portrait chips and the dashed line fade out as the zones spread over them. Nothing hangs on after its point is made.
2. **The zoom carries labels off.**
   - In the drill-down, the outer labels (PARTY, CENTRAL COMMITTEE, POLITBURO) stay where they were written in the crowd's space.
   - The camera's push slides them, cropped, off the frame edges.
3. **The slide changes through black,** and the archive run ends by a hard cut back to present-day footage.
4. **Graphics leave by a hard cut on the word.** Map to footage, card to painting, painting to hand.

## Our rules from this study
These are added to the Vox rules (tag after the thing, leave in reverse, never a plain fade).
- A thing enters the way it moves in life, and does its one action only after it lands.
- The camera never holds dead still on a map; something always moves.
- Old marks leave when the new mark makes them redundant.
- Labels written in a picture's own space are left behind by the camera's move, not faded.
- Archive runs go in a gate, with a slide change through black.
