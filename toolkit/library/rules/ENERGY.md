# Energy rules (Sam, 25 Nov 2026)

Sam: "the video is not as appealing as I would like it to be ... somewhere some kind of stimulation is missing";
"Build the stimulated version"; on the test of act 5 shots I-K: "Yes, this direction" (make it the house style and
rebuild act 5 this way); ground: "A · Studio Cream" (LOK-033) in place of the dark graphite.
These rules replace the calm limits of 22 Nov 2026 wherever they conflict. Accuracy, tags and the registry stay.

1. **Energy curve per act.** Every act plan draws its curve: quiet stretches, and 2-3 bursts at the act's turns
   (3-5 fast changes in 2-4 s with a sound hit, then a held beat). No stretch of 20 s at one intensity.
2. **Key words land big.** The word a turn hangs on lands as a large chip (90-150 px), accent or ink, slammed in
   (fast in, no fade), on its spoken word. Small mono tags stay for sources and labels only.
3. **Colour fills the frame.** Data colours (a swatch, the spectrum, a petal's pink) are shown big: full-frame floods,
   bands across the frame, tables at 1.2x. No important colour as a small strip on an empty ground.
4. **Every scene change moves.** Prefer the transitions that carry meaning (TRN-019 prism, TRN-020 page turn,
   TRN-021 colour flood, TRN-022 macro dive, morph, push); hard cuts only inside a burst.
5. **Camera moves you can feel.** LayerStage push up to its cap with pans of 150-250 px; drift on still shots may
   reach 6%.
6. **Sound lands.** A sub impact (SFX-025) on the act's one big line; a riser (MUS-015) into the act's turn; ticks or
   pops on items that count up or are named; the music swells 3-4 dB for 2-3 s at a reveal.
7. **Ground: LOK-033 Studio Cream** (warm cream, slow colour fields) for diagram, chip and data shots; pages keep
   the paper desk; photos and footage keep their own frames.
8. **The boredom pass.** At Gate 2 and Gate 3 the Editor also answers: "Where would a viewer reach for their phone?"
   Every stretch it names gets a change before Sam sees the clip.
