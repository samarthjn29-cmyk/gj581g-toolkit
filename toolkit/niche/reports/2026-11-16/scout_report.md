## The top five

### 1. The year we lost 11 days: the most time-critical pick
- **Working titles:** "The Year We Deleted 11 Days" / "Why Your Calendar Is Wrong" / "Britain Skipped 11 Days. Nobody Rioted."
- **Story per chapter (leads):**
  - Caesar's 445-day "year of confusion" (46 BC);
  - Gregory XIII's reform (1582: 10 days cut in Catholic Europe);
  - Britain and its colonies jump from 2 to 14 Sept 1752, and New Year moves from 25 March to 1 January;
  - Russia 1918 (the "October" Revolution happened in November).
  - Coda: Newton was born on Christmas Day 1642, or on 4 January 1643. A sibling chapter or short could ask why there are 7 days in a week.
- **Hero object:** a wall calendar torn from the 2nd straight to the 14th.
- **Peg:** Newton 25 Dec and New Year, both fixed. Five to six weeks from today. To land by about 22 Dec, a script has to start this week.
- **Evidence (measured):** "why do we have 7 days in a week" 548k US / 651k GB median; "history of the calendar" 114k, with autocomplete "history of calendar months"; the Erased Century small win (757k).
- **Countries:** UK, Ireland, US, Canada, Australia (1752); Europe (1582); India (many calendars still in use).
- **Risk:** the "give us our eleven days" riots are mostly a myth. Use it as the misconception beat, sourced.

- **Evidence (measured):** 622k/yr and 481k/yr seeds; 5 small-channel wins on "self aware" (including a 44k-subscriber clips channel at 3.4M, and a 73k channel at 430k with Penrose); Emergence 83.6x and 20.7x; Kurzgesagt 24M; Pursuit of Wonder's quantum-consciousness video at 5.3x.

## One bold bet: "The painter who hid the future" (who really invented abstract art?)
- **The story:** Hilma af Klint painted abstract canvases from 1906, years before Kandinsky's claimed "first abstract painting". She asked that they stay unseen for 20 years after her death. She, Kandinsky and Mondrian all died in 1944 (lead: verify).
- **Chapters (leads):**
  - the séance group "The Five";
  - *The Ten Largest* (1907);
  - the 1986 Los Angeles "The Spiritual in Art" show that brought her out;
  - the Guggenheim's 2018-19 show, its most-visited ever (lead).
- **The idea:** who gets credit for an idea, the first to have it or the first to show it?
- **Peg:** Artnet (10 Nov) says a rare af Klint painting could make auction history; a Kandinsky (*Betonte Ecken*, $60-80m) sells at Christie's on 17 Nov.
- **Why it's a bet:** demand is unmeasured. The seeds were added to the watchlist today, so next week's run will show it.
- **Hero object:** a giant pastel spiral canvas next to a tiny figure.
