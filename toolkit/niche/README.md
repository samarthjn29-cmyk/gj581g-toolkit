# Niche scout (skill: gj581g-niche-scout)
- `scout.py [watchlist.json] [outdir]` gathers signals into `reports/<date>/` (YouTube demand per seed query, YouTube and Google autocomplete, peer-channel outliers, Google Trends daily searches for IN/US/CA/GB, Google News for the watchlist topics) and writes `signals.md`.
- `watchlist.json`: the niches (core / testing / paused), their seed queries, peer channels, news topics, trend countries and subreddits. Edit it as the channel learns.
- `yt_search.py queries.txt out.csv`: ad-hoc YouTube search scrape (title, views, age, channel).
- Reddit blocks the workspace (HTTP 403), so the skill reads Reddit through WebSearch/WebFetch or the built-in browser.
- First full study: `channel/GJ581g_niche_insights.md` on G: (project `claude/NICHE_INSIGHTS.md`), 3 Nov 2026.
- **v2 (3 Nov 2026, global channel):** `search_geos` (US, GB, CA, AU, IN) searches each seed as a viewer in each country; week-over-week changes and new autocomplete terms against the last run's `metrics.json`; small-channel wins (videos from the last 12 months by channels under 100k subscribers with at least 50k views and more views than subscribers) as the gap measure; top viewer comments from peer outliers and core seeds (`comments.json`, through YouTube's web API); title-pattern tags and thumbnails (`thumbs/`) for outliers; the next 6 weeks from `calendar.json`.
- `calendar.json`: scheduled events (rate decisions, CPI releases, budgets, jobs reports, tax deadlines, summits) from official calendars, 4 Nov 2026 to 30 Jun 2027; `status` confirmed/expected; refresh monthly and extend it past June 2027.
- `alert.py`: the daily check. It stays silent unless a watched currency moves at least 1.5% in a day or 3% in 5 days, or makes its first 52-week extreme in a week (ECB daily reference rates, read per US dollar); or an alert phrase appears in a headline in the last 24 hours. It also notes tomorrow's scheduled events.
