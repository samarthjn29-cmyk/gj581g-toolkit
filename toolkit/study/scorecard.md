# Similar-videos scorecard (copy into the study; one row per video, plus ours)
Score 1-5, with one line of evidence per score. Score our own draft (script, animatic or clips) the same way before the script locks; any category where we sit below the best rival gets a fix, named in the study's SV-B list.

| Video | Hook (first 30 s) | Clarity (fresher could follow) | Visuals (devices, pace, no errors) | Humour (lands, on topic) | Fairness (both sides on charged points) | Sourcing (named, checkable) | Delivery (voice, pace, second voices) | Total /35 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Anchors:
- 5 = best seen in this study for that category; 3 = competent; 1 = a reason viewers left or complained (evidence: replay dips, comments).
- Hook: a concrete promise or surprise in the first 15 s, and something on screen changes within 3 s.
- Clarity: about 3 numbers a minute or fewer, a person before the concept, each term explained (our first-lecture rules are the yardstick).
- Visuals: replay peaks land on visual moments; no map, flag or AI errors; cut rate in the 20-30/min band for explainers.
- Fairness: the other side is stated in its own strongest words; opinions are attributed.
- Sourcing: sources named on screen or in the description; claims match them.
