### What's working
1. **At Emergence, the title and topic decide the result. The craft stays the same from video to video.** It posted 10 videos in about 30 days, all about 18-30 min, all in the same art and the same thumbnail style. Two of them got about 90% of the views:
   - *The Dark Knowledge of AI* (279k, **50x** its median of about 6k; channel has 5.8k subscribers). The shape is a **news hook plus a mysterious named idea**. The description opens with this month's news (a famous creator banned for "distillation"), then asks one question, then says "we explain distillation from scratch" through true stories (a sapphire, two Arctic expeditions, a children's puzzle-box study).
   - *Is It Just a Fancy Dictionary, or Is Something Emerging?* (71k, **13x**). The shape is a **debate question with two sides named**.
   - The plainer titles sit at 0.25-0.75x: "The Flaw at the Heart of AI", "How AI Solved The Wrong Game", "You're Hallucinating Right Now". The two newest are only 6 and 14 days old, so their numbers aren't final.
   - The two breakout shapes in the scout skill fit what we measured. Measured: views. Inferred: that the title shape is why.
2. **Emergence's thumbnail system:**
   - 9 of its 10 thumbnails share one house style: a dark slate ground, **one lit hero object** in the centre (a neural core streaming into a chip, an Othello board, a counter beside a food bowl), and a **2-4 word line that is different from the title**, with one word set apart ("Stealing the *mind*.", "Nobody *wrote* this.").
   - The one break from the style (bold red sans on a map, "TRILLION-DOLLAR MESS") still reached 2.1x.
   - So the style isn't the deciding factor. Small text plus one object reads well.
3. **Mind topics win across the idea peers:**
   - Consciousness, dreams and the quantum mind: Aperture 10.4x and 7.3x, Pursuit of Wonder 5.0x.
   - Attention and thinking in the age of phones and AI: TED-Ed 9.3x (4.6M), Aperture 5.5x.
   - Game theory and cooperation: Pursuit of Wonder 9.0x (2.7M).
   - "Named theory" titles keep appearing in these winners: "The ___ Theory of ___", "The theory of time that proves...".
   - Kurzgesagt's AI video is 4.3x at 23M views.
4. **Format hooks still pay.** Domain of Science's "The Map of ___" is 3 of its top 4 (5.7-9.7x). TED-Ed's 5-minute "you" lessons win on attention. "What if" titles average 1.47x over 8 videos this week (1.88x last week), which keeps supporting PW-03 (approved).
5. **Viewers reward visibly human-made art:**
   - TED-Ed's third comment, 2.0k likes: "thank you ted-ed for not turning into AI slop".
   - On Aperture: "refreshing to find content made by an actual human being".
   - On TED-Ed: "the artwork in these videos is always so beautiful".
   - Viewers are watching for AI-looking images, and the art itself earns likes.
6. **Packaging details on Emergence's two winners:**
   - Chapters are named "Chapter N: <name> (<the story inside>)", with a cold-open chapter first and a closing chapter that is a question ("Who does it belong to?", "What comes next?").
   - Descriptions run 3.5-4.3k characters, start with the hook story, and list sources.
   - The channel's pinned comment answers the video's question honestly ("nobody put it there...") and invites a reply.
   - Peers with sponsors (Aperture, Pursuit of Wonder, Domain of Science) mostly don't list sources. Emergence and we do.
7. **What viewers ask next, and their doubts:**
   - Domain of Science: "Please do one on fungi!!!" (4.3k likes).
   - Game theory: the top comment says real people aren't rational agents (7.2k likes), a ready objection beat for any game-theory story.
   - Emergence AI videos: comments argue about ownership ("why call it theft?", with a timestamp at 7:42). A closing debate question gets people commenting.
   - Aperture's dream video: the comments are personal stories (dreams of dead relatives, recurring dreams). Mind topics bring out personal stories, which build a community.
8. **Length and cadence:**
   - Emergence's winners are 19-20 min, and its 23-30 min uploads mostly underperform. This is a weak lead (n=10).
   - Aperture runs long (median 34 min). TED-Ed, minutephysics and School of Life run 4-6 min.
   - Emergence posts about 2-3 a week. TED-Ed posts 23 in 90 days. Domain of Science, OverSimplified and Philosophy Vibe posted nothing in 90 days and still have the highest per-video medians among the big channels (OverSimplified 39.5M).
   - So output volume isn't what wins for the large channels.
