# Decked out Data toolkit

The helper scripts behind the Decked out Data skills (research-story, deck-builder, media, youtube-packaging), so a new project reuses tested code instead of rebuilding it. Version 2.0, 28 Oct 2026: one-command pipeline and checkpoints. `run.py video.json all` runs story -> fact-check checklist -> narration -> retime -> media -> Remotion setup -> word timings -> assets -> generated scenes -> sections -> review sheets -> captions, stopping with a plain message at anything that needs a fix or a person (see "One-command pipeline" below). New: `remotion/gen_scenes.py` writes a working scene for every slide from slides.json (chapter maps, word cards, title, suspects, scorecard and full-screen clips are final; other layouts get their background, title, video panel, source line and red TODO boxes timed to the narration, to be replaced in `src/scenes_custom.jsx`, which always wins); `remotion/src/layouts.jsx` holds those layouts; `snapCue()` lands any build on the nearest spoken word; `remotion/review.mjs` + `review_sheet.py` render one still per scene with its narration line (the approval checkpoint before any full render); `story/factcheck.py` lists every on-screen and spoken claim with the source it cites, for an independent fact-check agent (voicing refuses while a row is unverified or failed); `media/library.py` keeps a channel-wide index of vetted free clips and photos (source, id, licence, proof, size, tags, where used) and re-downloads by id; the Remotion composition id is now `Video` and `render.bat` takes the project prefix. Kept in git (private GitHub repo) from 2.0. 1.9.1, 28 Oct 2026: `graphics/riso.py` turns photos and short clips into risograph prints (2-3 spot-colour inks as rotated halftone screens, ink grain, white flecks, misregistration, warm paper; `image` and `video` modes, about 0.5 s per 1080p frame). 1.9 (28 Oct): house style and a second voice, from two reference videos (Maxinomics on data centers, Urbanomaly on apartments). `remotion/src/house.jsx` adds dot-matrix charts and maps (`DotColumns`, `DotBars`, `DotMap`), words behind a cut-out subject (`TextBehind`), labelled photos (`LabelledPhoto`), framed archive clips with a year tag (`ArchiveFrame`), small source cards (`SourceCard`) and big numbers over footage (`BigNumber`); scripts can give short lines to a second 'viewer' voice with `{viewer: ...}` (`tts/synth.py` with `VIEWER=<voice id>`, `tts/align.py`, `story/vo_lib.py`, captions and .srt mark them). 1.8 (28 Oct): the video is now rendered directly with Remotion (`remotion/`, see `remotion/README_remotion.md`) instead of a PowerPoint deck: one frame-accurate timeline for narration, footage and builds; every build cued to the exact spoken word (`tts/align.py` recovers word timings from the Kokoro clips); burnt-in captions with word highlighting plus a YouTube .srt; paper reveals with a highlighter sweep; people and dot grids; split screens; 2.5D parallax stills (`media/parallax.py`); animated maps and event diagrams; paper texture, shadows and film grain. Renders in sections (cloud, 2 cores) or in one step on Sam's laptop (`remotion/render.bat`). Example: `examples/blindness_remotion/`. The PowerPoint pipeline below still works when a .pptx is wanted. 1.7.3 (28 Oct): per-slide narration. With `"narration_clips": "tts/vo"` in `deck_config.json`, `deck/tail.js` puts each slide's own VO-NN.mp3 on that slide (off-canvas, starting 0.2 s in, stopping at the slide's end) and `media/apply_timing_media.py` writes it as a full-volume audio play on the slide's timeline, so the voice can't drift from the slides; one continuous track on slide 1 drifted by every transition's length (PowerPoint plays transitions on top of the advance timers; seen on Blindness slides 15-16). No 'Play in Background' step any more. The legacy single track (`"audio"` alone) still works and is still mixed for the motion preview. 1.7.2 (28 Oct): smooth motion and transitions (after stutter on Blindness slides 18 and 34). `media/prep.py` keeps each slot's native frame rate (no 24->30 fps judder), slows a short clip by frame blending instead of repeating frames and lists anything slowed over 1.15x, and adds a 1 s tail (held last frame) so no clip ends before its slide; new `media/pushin.py` renders photo push-ins with sub-pixel smooth motion (ffmpeg zoompan steps in whole pixels); `media/apply_timing_media.py` writes 0.5 s fades as PowerPoint's 'fast' speed (was 'medium', 0.75 s). 1.7.1 (28 Oct): `media/prep.py` smooth starts: the cover (PowerPoint's poster frame, shown during the slide transition) is now the clip's first frame, not the frame at 1 s, so playback no longer jumps back when it starts (seen on Blindness slide 34); `examples/blindness_v4/` adds the 2026 registered-report slide (exact-scale sample-size bars). 1.6 (27 Oct): keyless media sources (Pexels and Pixabay keys aren't available): `media/coverr.py` (Coverr stock video), `media/openimages.py` (Cleveland Museum of Art CC0, The Met public domain, NASA photos), `graphics/icons.py` (Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor icons recoloured to the palette) and `graphics/mapmaker.py` (Natural Earth choropleth maps);   thumbs/               <Prefix>_thumb_A.html ... -> .png, sheets

## One-command pipeline (2.0)
`video.json` in the project folder:
```
{"prefix": "Homes", "project": "project.py", "voice": "am_adam", "viewer": null, "speed": 1.1, "dialect": "us",
 "kokoro_dir": "<path to the voice model>", "media_specs": "media_specs.json", "remotion": "Homes_remotion", "snips": "snips",
 "browser": "/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1243/chrome-linux64/chrome"}
```
| Command | Does | Stops when |
|---|---|---|
| `python3 toolkit/run.py video.json story:factcheck` | hand-off files, then `story/<Prefix>_factcheck.csv` | handoff errors |
| (agent) | an independent fact-check agent fills `verdict` / `fix` for every row against the sources | |
| `run.py video.json voice:sections` | narration, retime, prepare clips, Remotion project, word timings, assets, generated scenes, sections | a fact-check row unverified / WRONG / UNSUPPORTED / OVERSTATED; a word-timing mismatch; `{viewer:}` lines without a viewer voice |
| (Claude) | replace the TODO boxes: copy each scene from `src/scenes_gen.jsx` into `src/scenes_custom.jsx` and design it (house style) | |
| `run.py video.json review` | `out/<Prefix>_review_N.png`: one still per scene + narration line. **Send to the user and wait for approval** | |
| `run.py video.json srt:package` | YouTube captions, laptop package (zips < 30 MB, render.bat, README) | a zip over 30 MB |
| `run.py video.json render` | cloud render in sections + narration + grain | any TODO box left |
Stage names: story, factcheck, voice, retime, media, setup, words, assets, gen, sections, review, srt, package, render (`a:b` runs a range; `all` = story to srt).

Media library: `python3 media/library.py search office night --kind video` before searching the web; `library.py add ...` for every newly vetted asset; `library.py fetch <key>`; `library.py seed media/licence_log.csv --project <Prefix>` after a video.

## What each script guarantees
- `story/handoff.py`: checks runtime (4-12 min), that a `hypotheses` slide comes in the first quarter and is answered by a later `scorecard` with the same theories, reports the average gap between visual changes (aim 3-4 s), one number per sentence, no digits in speech, banned phrases and subscribe asks, title length, snippet length, Media line on every card, visual gaps over 5 s (builds and background cuts count), unknown source IDs, and reminds you when there are fewer than 2 word cards, chapter maps or silent holds. Slides get durations from the word estimate or, after narration, from the real clip lengths, plus any silent `hold`.
- `deck/head.js`: no slide numbers; optional `"texture": "grid"` in `deck_config.json` puts a faint grid behind white content slides (`deck/texture.py`, run by `build.sh`); palette from `deck_config.json` (seven roles plus `light_accent`, `mid`, `line_on_media` for media slides), layouts `CORP_CONTENT`, `CORP_TITLE`, `CORP_MEDIA`, and helpers: `frame`, `A` (timed builds), `T`, `R`, `L`, `kpi`, `col`, `chev`, `paper`, `paperMedia`, `mediaHead`, `VID` (clip + autoplay; matte box in preview builds), `snipPanel`, `tag`, `strip`, `quoteMark`, `footnote`, `wordCard(n0, {video?})` (full-screen words at a climax, revealed line by line) `mapSlide(n0, {video?})` (chapter map: current item in Callout, past items dimmed, hidden items as ??????), and since 1.3 `evidenceCard(s, snipKey, {x, y, w, rotate?, cite?})` (source screenshot card with shadow; returns `card` and `arrow` names for builds), `pill(s, text, {...})` (capsule label for mechanism diagrams), `hypotheses(n0, {video?})` and `scorecard(n0, {video?})` (theories ranked by evidence with 0-3 dots).
- `deck/tail.js`: matte builds never overwrite `timing.json`.
- `media/apply_timing_media.py`: transitions, auto-advance, build animations and the `play` effect. Clips start by themselves: **confirmed in real PowerPoint (Oct 2026)**.
- `media/prep.py`: slow motion up to 1.8x, then loops; lists looping slides (visible cut on restart); tint in the palette's Primary; `src` as a list cuts 2-3 clips into one background (evenly, or at `cut_at` seconds) so the picture changes every few seconds.
- `media/mixkit.py`: refuses Restricted (non-commercial) items; picks 1080p where available.
- `media/coverr.py`: free (non-premium) clips only; skips vertical and AI-generated clips; Coverr licence = commercial use, no credit required.
- `media/openimages.py`: only CC0 (Cleveland), isPublicDomain (Met) or NASA items without third-party credits; logs the credit line.
- `graphics/icons.py`: ISC/MIT icon sets from GitHub, recoloured, rendered to transparent PNG with Playwright. `graphics/mapmaker.py`: Equal Earth projection, exact stated bins, hatched 'No data', Callout outline for highlighted countries.
- `media/openvideo.py`: accepts only public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA (never NC, ND, 'fair use' or unknown); Internet Archive items only from trusted collections (Prelinger, FedFlix, National Archives) unless `--any`; NASA items credited to third parties are skipped; every download and every user-supplied clip gets a row in `media/licence_log.csv` with the credit line. Never downloads from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X or news sites.
- `tts/align.py`: word timings exact at every pause (each segment is re-synthesised; Kokoro on CPU is deterministic), spread by phoneme count inside a segment and snapped to energy dips (about +/-0.1 s); prints OK or MISMATCH per clip.
- `media/parallax.py`: subject cut-out (soft edge) and an inpainted background so the layers can slide without a hole.
- `tts/synth.py`: pauses in the markup become exact silences; reports words missing from the dictionary (add them with `PRON=pron.json` or to `CUSTOM` in `g2p.py`).

## Examples
`examples/themes/`: three theme presets (theme_retro.json, theme_vintage.json, theme_modern.json: theme, texture, palette and footage look to paste into deck_config.json / media_specs.json) and themes_preview.png (the v13 test slides in each).
`examples/v13_features/`: a 4-slide test of the 1.3 helpers (hypotheses, evidence card with arrow, pills, scorecard, grid texture) with a test PDF.
`examples/blindness_remotion/`: the Blindness v6 video scenes (scenesA/B/C.jsx: map zoom and chase diagram hook, parallax gorilla with 100-figure grid, paper reveals, split screen night vs day, 1,000-dot sample sizes, scorecard) and their data files.
`examples/blindness/`: the complete Blindness project inputs (project.py, slides.js, deck_config.json, media_specs.json), the opener and experiment animations, and the thumbnail renderer with a reusable flat gorilla silhouette.
