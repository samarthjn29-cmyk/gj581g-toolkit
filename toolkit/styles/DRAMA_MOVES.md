# Dramatic picture and video moves (Moctar, 14 Nov 2026)

Sam, 14 Nov 2026: "Are there any new type of picture or video gags which we can use. I want the videos to be a bit dramatic."
Shapes from film and documentary editing guides (Nikon "10 tricks", PremiumBeat suspense tips, Doc Film Academy), rebuilt for
painted stills. None is built yet unless marked; each is one camera move, card or sound habit in the stills kit.

| # | Move | What it does on a still | Built from | Fit in Moctar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Dolly zoom (vertigo) | subject layer stays the same size while the background layer stretches away | layers.py depth split | ct3 "there's a catch" |
| D2 | Drop to silence + slow push | bed and room tone cut out 0.5 s before the line, a slow push-in, one low hit after | mix_bed --mute + push | ct6 "He couldn't get one." |
| D3 | Colour isolation | the picture drains to grey except one object, which stays in colour | mask + grade | ct6 / en2 the homemade guitar |
| D4 | Match cut | cut between two pictures that share a shape at the same place on screen | two stills, aligned | en2 homemade guitar -> real stage photo |
| D5 | Letterbox | black cinema bars slide in for the big moment, out after | new card | gt14 "Twice." / en1 |
| D6 | Rack focus | background sharp then blurs as the subject sharpens (or the reverse) | layers.py + blur | al5 brain -> page |
| D7 | Speed ramp | slow drift, then a sudden snap-in on the key word | new move 'ramp' | ct9 "Same old dog." |
| D8 | Slow pull-back reveal | start on a detail, pull back to show what it belongs to | pullback + focus (built) | used: al3 costume |
| D9 | Withheld payoff | cut away before the answer, return to it later with the answer | plan only | ct7-ct9 AI stories |
| D10 | Long hold | stay on a picture 1-2 s past the line, no music | plan + mute | en1 "Some of them are very real." |
