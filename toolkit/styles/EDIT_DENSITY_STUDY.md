# Edit density study: how heavily edited channels use effects and sounds (11 Nov 2026)

Sam asked for this study (item 6 of 11 Nov 2026) to grow the effects library. **Method:** in the browser pane, one minute of each video was sampled at one frame a second (60 frames, contact sheet), and each visible change was counted: a cut, a picture or text appearing, an overlay, a sticker or a zoom. For Climate Town the full transcript (with its [music], [screaming] and [groaning] cues) and the top 60 comments were read as well. One minute per video is a sample, not an average, so treat the counts as rough.

## What this tells us
1. **We are already as busy as the busiest channels** (about 33 changes a minute, Slayy Point about 35). The gap is not quantity. It is kinds of gag and how long each one stays readable: every one of these channels leaves the joke on screen long enough to get it, which is our 2-second and reading-time rule.
2. **The joke is shown literally, as an object.** Climate Town's "a cell phone on 11% battery" is a real phone popping in. Slayy Point's price is a sticker that stays. That is the strongest shape here, and our script has many candidates: "zero alien contacts" (an empty contacts list), "the bus fare" (a bus ticket sticker), "a bake sale" (a cake stall with a price card).
3. **Sticker objects are the house texture of Indian vlog editing** (Slayy Point): cut-out objects with a white border pop in, sit beside the subject and leave. That is cheap to make (cut-outs through the Adobe background removal) and reads as Sam's own culture.
4. **Gag stings last about one second and stack pictures** (Climate Town's "DANG!"): a burst of three or four images plus one word, then straight back. Right after a big claim.
5. **Speech bubbles and escalating text carry the jokes when there is no host on camera** (OverSimplified). For stills videos that is the closest thing to a performer: give the pictures their lines.
6. **Documents come in as objects, then the key line is set as big text** (Climate Town, also Johnny Harris). That is better than a full-screen page for a quick citation.
7. **Raw texture comes from mistakes left in**: Climate Town keeps the camera operator's lost shoe ("RIP camera man's shoe" is a top comment) and runs bloopers after the credits. Our version is Sam's real outtakes after the "Next time" card.

## Added to the library from this study
- `sticker`: a cut-out object with a white border pops in, wobbles and stays (Slayy Point). Paired sound: pop.
- `burst`: a one-second collage sting of 3-4 pictures plus one big word (Climate Town's "DANG!"). Paired sound: impact or air horn.
- `grow`: the spoken words get bigger word by word while the camera crashes in (OverSimplified). Paired sound: rising whoosh, then an impact.
- `doc`: a document slides in from the side, then its key line is lifted out as big text (Climate Town). Paired sound: paper whoosh.
- Already in the library and confirmed by the study: pip (corner inset), freeze (black and white), tag (price sticker), bubble, lens, banner and headline.

## Rules
- Literal objects beat abstract graphics: when a line names a thing, show the thing.
- One gag sting (burst) per chapter at most. It's a spice.
- Keep other channels' footage, characters and bits out: we take shapes only. News clips like Climate Town's need the commentary-use rules in `gj581g-stills-world`.
