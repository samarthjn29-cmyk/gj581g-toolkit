# Our own gags from web research (Moctar, 14 Nov 2026)

Sam, 14 Nov 2026: "create your own gags by getting ideas from internet searches and utilizing them in the video."
Each idea takes a **shape** from a named source and is built only from our own painted stills, kit cards and sounds.
Nothing copies another creator's clip, character or catchphrase.

## Shapes found
| Shape | Source | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Switch image | Noël Carroll's sight-gag taxonomy, via [Wikipedia: Visual gag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_gag) | a later view shows what the first view hid, and the meaning flips (a pull-back reveal) |
| Object analog / mimed metaphor | same | an object used as if it were something else, played straight |
| Solution gag | same | a problem solved with a surprising physical fix |
| Sudden music pause | [AIR Media-Tech, editing tricks](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/editing-tricks-for-fast-paced-funny-videos) | the bed stops dead half a beat before the punchline |
| Hold a beat too long | same | stay on the picture after the line ends, then cut |
| Subverted rule of three | same ("subverted expectations") | two straight items, the third breaks the pattern |
| Toy stand-in | Phil Edwards, via [The Explainers](https://theexplainers.substack.com/p/making-the-trivialnontrivial-how) | a toy plays the big event, deadpan |
| Self-deprecating aside | same | the narrator's own on-screen jab at himself |

## Built for Moctar (all painted, all on lines Sam has locked)
1. **Switch image, al3 "an Earth animal in a costume".** Tight on two green antennae and a wild alien outline; pull back on "costume" to the whole dog in a cheap alien suit (`A_costume`, move pullback, focus on the antennae). No caption needed.
2. **Peel stamp (7th study `peel`), bt4 "Cerf never paid him".** Stamp "PAID?" peels to "NOT PAID", then to "NOT A SINGLE DOLLAR". Rips on each peel, sad trombone at the end. The words follow the line exactly; "bloody" stays spoken only.
3. **Sudden music pause, bt4.** The bed stops 0.5 s before "Not a single bloody dollar" and comes back after it (`mix_bed.py --mute`).
4. **Subverted rule of three, ct5 "A little pressure ... Too much ...".** On the gauge: labels "a little: inventive", "more: focused", then the needle jumps into the red with "too much: nap time". It sits on the real finding (helps, then hurts).
5. **Toy stand-in, ct4 "up to a point".** A painted wind-up toy hiker marches up the hill-shaped curve and topples off the far side. This replaces the drawn walker.
6. **Hold a beat too long, al2 "and so did most of Ward's students".** Stay on the wall of drawings 0.8 s after the line, with crickets, then cut.
7. **Self-deprecating aside, op5 "this question which I had long forgotten".** A small aside: "(he had. completely.)". The narrator is joking at himself, nobody else.
8. **Object analog, al5 "a page that's already got a dog on it".** The blank page is played straight as a dog bed: the sketched dog curls up on it. A painted still, no caption.

Rules kept: no real person's likeness in a gag; spice 2 at most; captions of 8 words or fewer; no punchline shown before it is spoken; every gag sits on a fact that stays true.
